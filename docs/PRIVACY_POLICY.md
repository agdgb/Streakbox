# Privacy Policy for Streakbox

**Last Updated:** August 19, 2026  
**Effective Date:** August 19, 2026

Streakbox ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure for the **Streakbox** mobile application.

---

### 1. 100% Local-First Architecture

In Streakbox Version 1.0:
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location, or contact list.
- **Local Storage Only:** All habit data, check-in timestamps, notes, and custom settings are stored locally on your device in an encrypted local database (SQLite).
- **No External Analytics or Advertising Trackers:** Streakbox V1 contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero behavioral trackers, and zero telemetry monitors.
- **Offline by Design:** The app functions 100% offline without requiring an internet connection or account creation.

---

### 2. Data Backup & Export

- **Manual Backups:** When you choose to export your habit data, Streakbox generates a structured JSON backup. This backup remains entirely in your custody and control.
- **No Cloud Uploads:** Backups are never transmitted to our servers or third-party servers unless you explicitly share or save them to your own personal cloud drive (e.g. Google Drive, local storage).

---

### 3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

- Streakbox offers optional premium features through Google Play In-App Billing.
- All payment transactions, subscriptions, and billing data are processed exclusively and securely by Google Play. We do not receive, store, or process your credit card numbers or banking details.

---

### 4. App Permissions

Streakbox requests only essential device permissions required to deliver core functionality:
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely to schedule local habit reminders and streak milestone alerts on your device. Notifications are scheduled locally without sending any data over the internet.
- **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Used to trigger habit reminders accurately at your chosen anchor time.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

Streakbox does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we introduce cloud synchronization or authenticated user accounts in future major releases (such as V2.0), this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those features, and users will be provided with transparent notice and explicit opt-in controls.

---
