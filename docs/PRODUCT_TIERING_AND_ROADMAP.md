# 🧠 Step-by-Step Implementation Roadmap

We will implement this in distinct, high-quality phases:

### Phase 1: 📱 Social Media Share Cards (100% Free)
- Generate sleek, shareable visual snapshot cards (Habit Streak, 12-Month Matrix, Milestone Achieved).
- Uses `RepaintBoundary` + native share sheet to instantly share to Instagram Stories, WhatsApp, Twitter/X, and gallery.

### Phase 2: 🔔 Behavioral Psychology Notification Engine
- **Trigger-based Psychological Nudges**:
  1. *Loss Aversion*: "You're on day 29 of 30. Don't let your month of progress reset tonight!"
  2. *Momentum Booster*: "Only 2 days until you crush your all-time best streak!"
  3. *Implementation Intention*: Morning focus prompt tied to the habit's optimal time of day.
- Fully configurable with quiet hours and smart frequency capping.

### Phase 3: ☁️ Google Sign-In & Cloud Storage Sync
- Step-by-step Google Sign-In / OAuth integration.
- Encrypted Google Drive App Data folder or cloud database sync (with automatic conflict resolution).

### Phase 4: 👑 Pro Tier Architecture & Paywall Showcase
- Subtle, non-intrusive Pro badges on advanced analytics and themes with a modern, high-converting paywall preview sheet.
